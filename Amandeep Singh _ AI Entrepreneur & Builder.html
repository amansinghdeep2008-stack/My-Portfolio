About Me

Introduction

Hi! I'm Amandeep Singh, an aspiring AI Entrepreneur from New Delhi, India, passionate about building AI-powered solutions that solve real-world business problems. I believe Artificial Intelligence is more than just technology — it's an opportunity to transform how businesses operate, grow, and innovate. My mission is to turn AI capabilities into practical business solutions through intelligent automation, AI-powered applications, and emerging technologies.

Vision

To build a high-impact AI company that makes Artificial Intelligence more accessible, practical, and valuable for businesses, entrepreneurs, creators, and organizations.

Interests

My Interests : 🔹 AI Entrepreneurship — Building towards an AI-powered agency focused on helping businesses leverage AI. 🔹 Generative AI & Prompt Engineering — Exploring the potential of modern AI models and intelligent applications. 🔹 AI Automation — Creating smarter workflows that reduce repetitive work and improve operational efficiency. 🔹 AI-Powered Development — Building applications, websites, digital products, and AI-driven solutions using modern development tools. 🔹 No-Code / Low-Code Development — Turning ideas into functional products through AI-assisted development. 🔹 Business Problem-Solving — Identifying opportunities where AI and automation can create meaningful value. I’m particularly interested in connecting with: AI Entrepreneurs & Founders Business Owners & Startup Teams AI Developers & Automation Experts Technology Innovators People building with Generative AI 💡 Have a business problem that AI or automation could solve? Let's connect and explore what's possible. Let's build something meaningful with AI. Learning AI. Building with AI. Creating businesses powered by AI. 🚀
